    About RDx

    RDx is a badminton community of 300+ players. We play every morning and evening on our own courts, members and drop-in players together, and rotate so everyone gets games.

    How a session goes

    1. Vote for your batch in the app
    2. Come to the academy
    3. Play on your batch's courts
    4. Rotate so everyone gets games
