    How a day at RDx works

    Vote for your slot

    Pick the morning or evening session in the RDx app. Confirm from 12 hours before; back out up to 30 minutes before the start.

    Members or drop-in

    Members pay once for a set of sessions on fixed days, or keep a flexible wallet for any day. Everyone else pays per session.

    Bring your friends

    Add friends when you vote. They pay the session fee, and courts are shared so everyone gets games.

    The people who run it

    Call or message any of us to join, ask about plans, or enter a tournament.

      Join in four steps

      1. Message usWhatsApp or call anyone from the team with your name.
      2. We add youYour number is added to the RDx app, as a drop-in player or with a plan.
      3. Sign inOpen the app, enter your number and choose a 4-digit PIN. Your phone stays signed in.
      4. Vote and playPick a slot, confirm on the day, and tap "I played" after the game.
