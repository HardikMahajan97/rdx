Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, where it is stored, how we protect it, and your rights under India's data protection law.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how RDx BADDY GROUP handles your personal data, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000. It covers this website, the RDx booking app, and photos and videos taken at our sessions and events.

1. Who is responsible for your data

RDx BADDY GROUP ("RDx", "we", "us"), a badminton community run by Rahul Dhekane, decides why and how your personal data is used. Under the DPDP Act, that makes us the Data Fiduciary, and you, the person the data is about, the Data Principal.

Questions, requests and complaints about your data go to our Grievance Officer (see section 13).

2. What personal data we collect

You give us

  • Registration details: first and last name, mobile number, email address, skill level and gender.
  • Your PIN: stored only as a salted, one-way hash. Nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Plan requests and messages: the plan you ask for and anything you tell us when you contact us.

Created when you use the app

  • Activity: the sessions you book, confirm, cancel and play, no-shows, your plan, its balance and its history.
  • Payment records: amount, date, status and transaction ID. We never receive your UPI PIN, card number or bank details.
  • Preferences: the language you choose.
  • Technical data: sign-in cookies, and basic technical data (such as your IP address and the time of access) used for security, for example to block repeated wrong PINs.

Photos, videos and audio

  • Photos and videos (which may include sound) of play, tournaments and events, taken by the RDx team (see section 6).

We do not use your phone's camera, microphone, contacts or location. The app never asks for them. The short intro video on our home page only plays to you; nothing is recorded from your device.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

We use your data only for the purposes below. We ask for your consent when you register, and you can withdraw it at any time (section 8).

  • Running your account: approving your registration, signing you in, and keeping your account secure.
  • Bookings and courts: showing the team who is coming, planning courts and rotation, and checking who you are at the court.
  • Plans, fees and refunds: managing your plan balance, collecting fees and paying refunds.
  • Contacting you: by phone, SMS or WhatsApp about your bookings, session changes, cancellations and community announcements.
  • Photos and videos: sharing the community's games and events on our News page and social media (section 6).
  • Legal and safety needs: keeping accounts and payment records as the law requires, handling disputes, and preventing fraud or misuse. The DPDP Act allows some of this without consent (as "legitimate uses").

We collect only what these purposes need, and we never use your data for anything unrelated without asking you first.

4. Who can see your data

  • The RDx admin and core team see players' details, and use them only to run the community.
  • Members can see the names of the players booked for the same session, on that session's court board. Other players never see your mobile number, email or payment details.
  • Service providers (Data Processors) that we use to run the website. Each one handles data under its own terms, which require it to keep the data secure:
    • Vercel: hosts the website and app, and provides anonymous visit statistics;
    • Neon: hosts our database;
    • Cloudinary: stores the photos and videos posted on our News page;
    • PhonePe: processes online payments;
    • WhatsApp and your mobile network, when we message or call you.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it, such as a court order or a lawful request from a government agency.

We never sell your personal data, and we never share it with advertisers or marketers.

5. Where your data is stored

Our database is hosted in Singapore, and our service providers may process data on servers outside India. The DPDP Act allows this, except to countries the Government of India restricts; we will stop any transfer the Government restricts. Wherever it is stored, your data is protected as described in this policy.

6. Photos, videos and audio

  • The RDx team may take photos and videos, which may include sound, at sessions, tournaments and events, to share the community's games on our News page and social media.
  • You can say no. Tell any team member before or during a session that you do not want to be filmed or photographed, and we will keep you out of our photos and videos.
  • You can ask us to take it down. If a photo or video of you is on our website or social media and you want it removed, contact us. We will remove it from our website as soon as we can. Copies that others have already shared or saved are outside our control.
  • We only post photos and videos of players at play or at events. We never use them for advertising by anyone else, or for face recognition.
  • Players under 18 are photographed or filmed for posting only with a parent's or guardian's permission.
  • Other players' privacy: if you take photos or videos at a session, please respect anyone who does not want to be recorded, and do not post other players' contact details or private moments.
  • Any CCTV at the venue is run by the venue under its own policy, not by RDx.

7. Cookies and storage on your phone

  • Necessary cookies: keep you signed in, remember your language, and hold the short-lived PIN step. The app cannot work without them.
  • Your browser's storage: remembers that you have seen the intro video, so it plays only once.
  • Visit statistics (Vercel Web Analytics) count page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
  • We use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Log out (top right of the app) deletes all RDx cookies and stored data from your browser.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Access: get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correction and completion: have wrong or incomplete details fixed. You can change your name yourself in the app.
  • Erasure: have your personal data deleted when it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it for, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before, and we can then no longer run your account, so we will close it.
  • Grievance redressal: have your complaint answered by us (section 13).
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act.

To use any right, contact the Grievance Officer from your registered mobile number, or tell us that number so we can confirm it is you. Using your rights is free.

9. Your duties

The DPDP Act also asks you to give true details, not to pretend to be someone else, not to hide important information, and not to make false or frivolous complaints.

10. How long we keep your data

  • Account details: while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, or when it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise your personal details, unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Booking and payment records: as long as tax and accounting laws require, after which they are deleted or anonymised.
  • Photos and videos: while they are on our pages, or until you ask us to remove them.

11. How we keep it safe

  • All traffic to the website is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • PINs are stored as salted hashes. Wrong PIN tries are limited, and resetting a PIN signs that account out everywhere.
  • Only the admin and core team can see players' details, each with their own PIN, and team access locks after 20 minutes idle.
  • Payments go through PhonePe; card and bank details never reach us.

If a breach happens: if your personal data is leaked or accessed without permission, we will tell you without delay: what happened, what it may mean for you, what we are doing about it, and what you can do. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

12. Children

You must be 18 or older to register yourself. A player under 18 must be registered by a parent or guardian, who gives consent for them. We do not track children's activity, profile them or show them advertising.

13. Grievance Officer and complaints

For any question, request or complaint about your personal data, contact our Grievance Officer:

  • Name: Rahul Dhekane, community head
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +91 88981 17019
  • Hours: every day, 7 AM to 9 PM

We will reply and resolve your request within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you in the app or on WhatsApp, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
