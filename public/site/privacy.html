Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how we keep it safe.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who is responsible

This website and the RDx booking app are run by RDx BADDY GROUP, a badminton community run by Rahul Dhekane. Questions about your data can go to the contacts on our Contact Us page.

What we collect

  • Registration details: your first and last name, mobile number, email address, skill level and gender.
  • Your PIN: stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form that nobody, including us, can read.
  • Activity: the sessions you book, confirm, cancel and play; your plan and its balance; and records of payments (amount, date and status).
  • Preferences: the language you choose (English, Marathi or Hindi).
  • Cookies: small cookies that keep you signed in on your phone and remember your language. We also use anonymous visit statistics to see which pages are used; these do not identify you.

Why we use it

  • To approve your registration and confirm your slot bookings.
  • To show the team who is coming, so courts and rotation can be planned.
  • To verify your identity at the court.
  • To manage your plan, fees and refunds.
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about sessions, changes and announcements.

Who can see it

  • Only the RDx admin and core team can see players' details, and only to run the community.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with third-party marketers.
  • Your data is stored with the service providers that host this website and its database, only so the website can work.

Payment data

We do not store your UPI PIN or banking details. All transactions are securely processed via the PhonePe Payment Gateway. We only receive the result of a payment (amount, date, status and transaction ID) so we can confirm your booking or plan.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while your account is in use. If your account is removed, it is hidden from every list and can no longer sign in; booking and payment records are kept as long as needed for our accounts.

Your choices

  • You can change your name in the app at any time.
  • You can ask us to correct your details, or to remove your account, by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

You can reach us any day between 7 AM and 9 PM. See Contact Us for phone numbers and where we play.
