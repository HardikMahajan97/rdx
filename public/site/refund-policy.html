Cancellation & Refund Policy

When you can cancel, what you get back, and how refunds are paid.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cancelling a booking

  • You can cancel (back out of) a booking in the app until 30 minutes before the session starts. Tap "Can't make it" (or "I'm a member but I can't come" / "I've paid but I can't come") on the session card.
  • If the booking used a plan session, that session goes back on your plan balance straight away.
  • If you paid the session fee, the full fee is refunded to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

When a booking is final

From 30 minutes before the session starts, the booking is final and cannot be cancelled. There is no refund, and a plan session is used, if you:

  • cancel less than 30 minutes before the start;
  • book in the last 30 minutes before the start (these bookings are final straight away); or
  • do not come to the session (a no-show).

If RDx cancels a session

If we have to cancel a session (for example, the courts are not available), any plan session used for it goes back on your balance, and any session fee you paid for it is refunded in full to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Membership plans

  • A plan (fixed or wallet), once activated, is not refundable, including for unused sessions.
  • Plan sessions can be used until the plan runs out or reaches its end date.
  • If you paid for a plan and it was never activated, the amount is refunded in full within 5–7 business days.

Failed or duplicate payments

  • Failed transactions: if a payment is deducted from your account but the booking or plan is not confirmed, the amount will automatically be refunded by your bank within 3–5 working days.
  • Paid twice by mistake: contact us with the transaction details and the extra payment is refunded to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

How to ask about a refund

Call or message us with your registered mobile number, the session date, the amount and the transaction ID. You can reach us any day between 7 AM and 9 PM. See Contact Us for phone numbers, email and address.
