Terms & Conditions

The rules for using this website and the RDx booking app. Please read them before you register or pay.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who we are

RDx BADDY GROUP ("RDx", "we", "us") is a badminton community of 300+ players. It is an unregistered community run by its founder and community head, Rahul Dhekane, as an individual, with the help of the core team (Sadanand Wagh, Pranit Devre and Jaideep Bisen).

By registering, booking or paying on this website, you agree to these terms.

2. What this website does

This website and the RDx booking app let players:

  • register for an RDx account;
  • book (vote for) a seat in the morning or evening batch in advance;
  • buy a membership plan (a fixed plan for one batch on set days, or a wallet of sessions usable in any batch);
  • pay session fees and plan amounts online; and
  • see announcements, tournaments and news from RDx.

3. Your account

  • Register with your real name, your own mobile number and a working email. The RDx admin and core team approve each registration before you can sign in.
  • You sign in with your mobile number and a 4-digit PIN. Keep your PIN private; you are responsible for bookings made from your account.
  • One account per person. If you forget your PIN, tap "Forgot PIN?" and the team will reset it.
  • You must be 18 or older to register yourself. A player under 18 must be registered by a parent or guardian, who accepts these terms and gives consent for the player's personal data on their behalf.
  • We may deactivate or remove an account that gives false details, is misused, or breaks these terms.

4. How booking works

  1. Book: open the app and vote for the batch you want to play. Seats are limited by the number of courts.
  2. Confirm: from 12 hours before the session, tap "I'm coming" so the team knows you will be there.
  3. Back out if you can't come: you can cancel in the app until 30 minutes before the session starts. After that the booking is final.
  4. Play: come to the courts, play, and tap "I came & played" in the app. If you don't come, the booking counts as a no-show and the session is still charged.

Members must also book each session. A fixed plan can only be used for its own batch and days; a wallet can be used in any batch on any day.

5. Fees and pricing

  • The session fee and plan prices are shown in the app before you book or pay. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹).
  • Players without a plan pay the session fee for each session they book. Friends you bring always pay the session fee.
  • A plan session is used when the booking becomes final (30 minutes before the start).
  • Prices are subject to change based on court availability and the venue's charges. A change applies to bookings and plans paid after it is announced, not to ones already paid.

6. Payments

Online payments are processed securely through the PhonePe Payment Gateway. We never see or store your UPI PIN, card details or bank details. A booking or plan is confirmed only after the payment succeeds.

7. Cancellations and refunds

In short: cancel in the app at least 30 minutes before the session and a plan session goes back on your balance, or a paid session fee is refunded within 5–7 business days. Bookings are final after that, and activated plans are not refundable. The full rules are in our Cancellation & Refund Policy.

8. Rules on court

  • Arrive on time. Be at the court before your session starts.
  • Wear non-marking shoes. Only non-marking sports shoes are allowed on court.
  • Bring your own racket.
  • Respect the rotation. Follow the rotation so everyone gets games, and be respectful to other players, the team and the venue staff.
  • Follow the rules of the venue where you play.

The team may ask a player who breaks these rules to leave the session, and may deactivate their account.

9. Your safety

Badminton is a physical sport. Play within your fitness and at your own risk. Look after your belongings; RDx is not responsible for injury, or for loss of or damage to personal belongings, at the venue.

10. Your personal data

  • We handle your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, as set out in our Privacy Policy, which is part of these terms.
  • When you register, you consent to us using your name, mobile number, email, skill level, gender, bookings and payment records to run your account, bookings, plans and payments, and to contact you about them by phone, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Your data is stored with our service providers (website hosting, database, media storage and PhonePe), including on servers outside India. We never sell it or share it with advertisers.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time through our Grievance Officer. Withdrawing consent closes your account, because we cannot run bookings without it.
  • Keep your details true and up to date, and do not use another person's number or identity.

11. Photos, videos and audio

  • The RDx team may take photos and videos, which may include sound, at sessions, tournaments and events, and post them on our News page and social media.
  • If you do not want to be photographed or filmed, tell a team member before or during the session and we will keep you out. You can also ask us at any time to remove a photo or video of you from our pages.
  • Photos and videos of players under 18 are posted only with a parent's or guardian's permission.
  • If you take photos or videos at a session yourself, respect players who do not want to be recorded, and do not post other players' contact details or private moments without their permission.
  • Photos, videos, text and logos on this website belong to RDx or to the people who made them. Please do not copy them for commercial use without asking us.
  • The website and app never use your phone's camera, microphone or location.

13. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Data protection complaints that we cannot resolve can be taken to the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Contact

You can reach us any day between 7 AM and 9 PM. See Contact Us for phone numbers and our Grievance Officer.
