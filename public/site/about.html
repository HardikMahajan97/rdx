About RD’x

A badminton community that plays every morning and evening.

300+members

Who we are

RD’x is a badminton community of 300+ players. We play every morning and evening on Aliens Badminton Academy's courts, members and drop-in players together, and rotate so everyone gets games.

How a session goes

  1. Vote for your batch in the app
  2. Come to the academy
  3. Play on your batch's courts
  4. Rotate so everyone gets games
