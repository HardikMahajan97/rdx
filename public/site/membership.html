Being a member

A plan pays for your seat. You still vote for each session, so the team knows who is coming.

Two kinds of plan

Fixed plan

One batch, on the days you choose: every day, weekdays, weekends or your own days. You vote for your own batch on your own days.

Wallet plan

A balance of sessions you can use in any batch, on any day. Top it up when it runs low.

Already registered? Ask for a plan from the app. The team tells you the amount, and your plan starts once the payment is received.

Who pays what

  1. Vote for a sloteveryone votes, members too
  2. Are you a member?Yes: your plan pays for your seat
  3. Not a member?You pay for that session
  4. Bringing friends?They pay the session fee
