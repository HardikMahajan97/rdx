Questions people ask

What is RDx BADDY GROUP (RDx Baddy)?

RDx BADDY GROUP, also called RDx Baddy, is a badminton community of 300+ players run by Rahul Dhekane. We play every morning and evening, members and drop-in players together, and book every session on this website.

Do I need a membership to play?

No. Drop-in players vote for a session in the app and pay the session fee for it. A membership plan pays for your seat instead.

How do I join?

Register on this website with your name, mobile number, email, skill level and gender. The RDx admin and core team approve it, then you log in with your mobile number and set a 4-digit PIN.

How do I book a session?

Log in and vote for the batch you want. From 12 hours before the start, tap "I'm coming" to confirm.

What if I can't come?

Back out in the app until 30 minutes before the session starts. A plan session goes back on your balance, and a paid session fee is refunded. After that the booking is final.

What membership plans are there?

A fixed plan covers one batch on the days you choose. A wallet plan is a balance of sessions you can use in any batch on any day, and you can top it up.

Can I bring a friend?

Yes. Friends you bring pay the session fee.

Where and when do you play?

Every morning and evening. The About page shows the current halls, timings and courts.
